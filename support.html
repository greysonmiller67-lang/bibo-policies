Getting started

Setting up the first time

Bibo asks you (the parent) for your child’s first name, approximate age, and a few interests, then asks one placement question: does your child mostly imitate sounds, or use whole sentences? This picks the starting point. There are no accounts and no sign-in — setup takes about a minute and everything stays on your device.

If you pick the wrong starting point, nothing is lost. Bibo adapts as your child practises, and you can start over from Parent → Settings → Restart onboarding.

How do I open the Parent Area?

Press and hold the “Hold to enter” button for about a second and a half, until the ring fills. A quick tap won’t open it — that’s deliberate. It keeps your child inside the play area, where there are no ads and nothing to buy.

More than one child?

Yes. Parent → Settings → Profiles lets you add separate profiles, each with its own progress and settings. Switch between them any time.

The microphone and speech practice

The mic isn’t working

Bibo listens through your device’s built-in speech recogniser (Apple Speech on iPhone/iPad, Google Speech Services on Android). If practice isn’t responding to your child’s voice:

  • Check that Bibo has microphone permission (and, on iPhone/iPad, speech recognition permission) in your device’s Settings.
  • On Android, make sure Google Speech Services is installed and your language pack is downloaded.
  • Check the device isn’t muted, and try somewhere quieter — background noise makes recognition much harder.

Bibo doesn’t recognise what my child says

This is the most common message we get, and it’s worth being honest about: speech recognition is built for adult speech. It will miss a child who is still learning to make a sound — that is a limitation of the technology, not a measure of your child.

That’s why every speech exercise in Bibo also has an “I did it!” button. You or your child can tap it, and practice continues exactly as if the mic had heard them. It is a completely valid way to use the app — some families use it all the time.

If you deny microphone permission entirely, Bibo doesn’t break. Every exercise simply falls back to the “I did it!” button.

If the mic ever becomes a source of pressure for your child, turn it off and use the button. Practice should feel like play. That matters more than any streak.

Progress and reports

Downloading a progress report

Parent → Report → Download PDF report creates a PDF on your device with sessions, minutes, and mastered skills. Your device’s share sheet then lets you email it, message it, or save it. It contains no voice recordings.

It’s designed to hand to a speech-language pathologist — bringing it to a first visit gives them weeks of context in a page.

Clearing progress

  • Parent → Settings → Reset learning progress clears progress, sessions, and wins (with a confirmation step).
  • Parent → Settings → Restart onboarding clears the profile and starts setup again.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything. Because all of it lives on your device, we can’t recover progress after a reset, an uninstall, or a lost device.

Making Bibo comfortable

Under Parent → Settings you can tune the app to your child’s sensory needs:

  • Animation: Full, Low, or Minimal — turn the movement right down if it’s distracting or overwhelming.
  • Voice speed: Normal or Slow.
  • Font: three rounded, easy-to-read typefaces.
  • Sound and haptics levels.

The Parent → Guides tab also has short, practical reading on supporting practice at home, including what to do during a meltdown.

Ads, tips, and purchases

Where are the ads?

Ads appear only inside the Parent Area, behind the press-and-hold gate. The part of the app your child uses has no ads at all. We request non-personalised ads only.

What is “Support Bibo”?

An optional tip jar in Parent → Settings. It is a voluntary thank-you to the developer and it unlocks nothing — Bibo is free, and every feature is available to everyone. There is no paid version, no subscription, and no locked content. Your child never sees it.

I want a refund for a tip

Tips are processed by Apple or Google, never by us, so refunds go through them:

  • iPhone / iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history

If a tip was charged but the thank-you never appeared, email us and we’ll help you sort it out.

Good to know

Is Bibo a replacement for speech therapy?

No, and we’d never claim otherwise. Bibo is built for the days between sessions — short, playful, repeatable practice. It cannot assess your child, and it cannot replace a speech-language pathologist. If you have concerns about your child’s speech, a professional can offer guidance no app can.

What language is Bibo in?

Bibo teaches English speech sounds, so the app itself is in English. The store listing is available in many languages, but the practice content is not translated — the sounds, syllables, and words are the lesson.

Does Bibo need the internet?

No. Practice works fully offline. A connection is only used to load ads in the Parent Area.

Privacy

Nothing about your child leaves the device: no accounts, no voice recordings, no progress uploaded anywhere. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
